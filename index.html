Professor Rater Pro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Current Date]

1. Information We Collect

Professor Rater Pro collects the following information:

  • Professor names from web pages you visit
  • Your selected college name (stored locally on your device)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Fetch professor ratings and reviews from RateMyProfessors.com
  • Display professor ratings within the extension popup
  • Remember your selected college for future use

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Professor Rater Pro does not store any personal user data on external servers. Your selected college name is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. We do not share any user data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Our extension uses RateMyProfessors.com to fetch professor ratings. Please refer to their privacy policy for information on how they handle data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
